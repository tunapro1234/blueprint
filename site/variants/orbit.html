01 / THE IDEA

Keep the work moving.
Keep the whole picture.

Give each agent its own space to work, then bring them together. Blueprint connects persistent terminal sessions with a shared hierarchy, a message queue, and a view of the whole fleet.

⌘

A place for every agent.

Named tmux sessions and separate worktrees. Independent work, with a structure you can see.

Messages that can wait.

Working agents keep their focus. Messages queue while they’re busy, ready for their next turn.
